# Why This Article

A lot is being written and said about burnout but I believe most takes are trying to impose a single explanation (and solution) onto all cases of burnout. I believe there is a lot more nuance to the problem and that there is probably just as much variation in the causes, effects, and solutions of burnout as there is in the lives, personalities, and motivations of software engineers.
